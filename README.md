# Ergonomic Cat Bed

A cat bed made of sections that move. Raise and lower them to shape the bed,
and the cat lying on it settles into whatever shape you make — sagging into a
dip, arching over a bump, lifting its head where the bed rises to meet it.

Every cat is drawn by hand, and it is the drawing itself that bends.

### **▶ [Open the app](https://ergocatbed.windnoise.org/)**

## How to use it

**Shape the bed**

- **Drag a section** up or down. Grab it anywhere along its column.
- **Hold `Shift` while dragging** to sweep across several sections at once and
  draw a whole contour in one motion.
- **Auto-fit to cat** shapes the bed for you.
- **Flatten** resets it. **Re-drop cat** drops the cat again.

**Choose a cat**

Three to pick from, and they want different beds.

- **Gizmo** — tall head, stepped shoulder, no tail in sight.
- **Noodle** — all angles, wedge head and a sharp raised haunch.
- **Mochi** — enormous, a dome of cat with a small head and a hanging tail.

Each comes in three sizes, from kitten to chonk.

**Read the score**

The number is how comfortable the cat is, out of 100, broken into three parts:

- **Support** — is the body held up, or bridging gaps?
- **Posture** — is the spine relaxed, or forced to bend?
- **Pressure** — is the weight spread out, or digging into one spot?

A flat bed scores poorly, and that is the point: every cat is drawn holding its
head clear of the ground, so a flat bed leaves the head unsupported. Shaping the
bed to catch it is the puzzle.

Turn on **Pressure map** to see where the weight actually lands, or **Ideal
contour** to see the line the cat's belly wants to rest on, then drag the
sections onto it.

**Keyboard**

`←` `→` select a section · `↑` `↓` nudge it · `F` flatten · `A` auto-fit ·
`Space` re-drop the cat
